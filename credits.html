Palace photograph & cinematic adaptations

“Palácio Queluz interior 1.JPG”, photographed by Husond, via Wikimedia Commons. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported.

The locally hosted files royal-hall.webp, palace-landscape.mp4, and palace-portrait.mp4 are adaptations: resized, cropped, color-graded, and, for the films, animated with a looping camera move. These adapted media files are also distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0. The photographer does not endorse this invitation.

The palace is an artistic backdrop, not a photograph of The Cove. The actual engagement venue is The Cove, Sector 5, Panchkula.

Original invitation artwork & animation

The embossed-paper ornament, wax seal, light effects, animated silk, particles, invitation typography, and page interactions were created for this invitation. The two supplied reference clips inspired the direction; their video, names, imagery, and music are not embedded or redistributed.

Fonts & music

Cormorant Garamond, Great Vibes, and Manrope are self-hosted under their included SIL Open Font Licenses in the assets/fonts folder. The optional instrumental music is an original synthesized music-box pattern, not a third-party recording.
